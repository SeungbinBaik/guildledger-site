Guild Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective: 9 September 2026

Summary

Guild Ledger runs entirely on your device. It has no account, no server, and no analytics, and it sends nothing to the developer or to any third party.

Information we collect

None. Guild Ledger collects no personal information. There is no sign-up, no advertising identifier, no analytics SDK, no tracking technology, and no code that transmits data over the network.

Information stored on your device

  • Your lives — the life you are playing and the register of the ones that closed are stored on the device.

That is all the app writes. It is never copied anywhere else.

Children's privacy

Because no user's personal information is collected, no child's personal information is collected either.

Generative AI

The artwork in this game, and some of its writing, was made with generative AI.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page. A change that begins collecting data would be announced explicitly alongside the app update.
