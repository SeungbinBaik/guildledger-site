Guild Ledger

You register at twenty. You are finished at sixty. Everything between is one life.

Support

Questions, bugs, or a save that will not open: seungbin.baik@gmail.com

If a save stops opening, do not delete the app before writing. The game keeps the previous copy on the device, and that copy can often be recovered.

Common questions

Where is the new game button? The register is the opening screen, and every screen has a door back to it. A life that has closed can be left there; a life still open is never overwritten.

Why does the card not show my chances? Because it never will. What you know about a beast changes what the card means, and a number would do the reading for you.

Does it need the internet? No. There is no account, no server, and no analytics.
